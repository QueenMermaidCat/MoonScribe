# MoonScribe 🌙

*A calm space for your thoughts.*

MoonScribe is a gentle, simple note-taking app for Windows — a quiet
little writing space with soft themes, a starry "Cosmic" background, and just
the right amount of formatting. No accounts, no cloud, no clutter. Just you and
your words.

---

## ⬇️ How to Download

1. Go to the [**Releases**](../../releases/latest) page.
2. Download **`MoonScribe.zip`** under "Assets."
3. Unzip it anywhere (for example, your Desktop).
4. Double-click **`MoonScribe.exe`** to start writing.

🔮 Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your
PC" notice the first time (this appears for any new app that isn't signed by a
big company); click **More info → Run anyway** to continue.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Four calm themes** — MoonScribe Dark, Light Mode, a starry Cosmic mode, and a
  dreamy Pastel Moon.
- **Tabs** — keep several notes open at once. They stay only for the current
  session, so every launch opens fresh and uncluttered.
- **Gentle rich text** — bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, alignment,
  bullet lists, fonts, sizes, text color, and highlight.
- **Emoji & symbol strips** — a curated row of emojis and celestial glyphs to
  drop into your notes.
- **Autosave & recovery** — while you have unsaved changes, MoonScribe quietly
  saves a backup every 60 seconds so an unexpected shutdown won't lose your work.
- **Spell check** — with right-click suggestions; toggle it on or off.
- **Your own format** — save notes as `.mnote`, or export to HTML, PDF, or
  plain text.
- **Live word & character count**, full-screen mode, and a resizable window.

---

## 💾 Saving your notes

MoonScribe saves in its own tidy `.mnote` format, which keeps all your
formatting exactly as you left it. You can also **Export** a copy as a web page
(HTML), a PDF, or plain text from the File menu when you want to share outside
the app.
